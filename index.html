Chinese Car Parts Wholesale & Export

Wholesale export supplier of replacement parts for Chery, Geely, BYD, Haval, MG, Changan, JAC and FAW vehicles. OE number or VIN quoting, consolidated containers.

Chinese-brand vehicles are now one of the fastest-growing vehicle parcs outside China. Chery, Geely, BYD, Haval, MG, Changan, JAC and FAW are exports in volume to Russia, the Middle East, Africa, Latin America, Southeast Asia and increasingly Europe. The service network has not kept pace with that parc, and parts identification is the bottleneck.

Qinparts is a wholesale export supplier built specifically for that gap. We consolidate replacement parts for Chinese-brand passenger cars and light commercial vehicles, quote against OE numbers or VIN, and ship consolidated orders to distributors, import-wholesalers, workshop chains and fleet operators.
