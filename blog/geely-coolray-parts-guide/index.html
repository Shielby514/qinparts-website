The three problems workshops hit

1. NoCross-reference coverage

Most international parts catalogues were built around Japanese, Korean, European and American platforms. Geely entered those catalogues late and unevenly, so a part number search returns nothing even where the part is abundant in China.

2. Platform sharing across marques

Geely Auto, Volvo, Polestar, Lynk & Co and Zeekr share architectures. CMA-derived components in particular cross between vehicles that no catalogue links together. Knowing which shared parts fit requires parts-side knowledge rather than a database lookup.

3. Market-specific trim codes

The same Coolray body carries different equipment by destination market. A Russian-spec car and a Mexican-spec car of the same year can differ in lighting, braking specification and suspension tuning, and those differences reach the parts level.

Our answer to all three is the same: quote against the VIN, not against the model name.
