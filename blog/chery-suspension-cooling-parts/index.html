Why these two categories specifically

Chinese-brand SUVs are, on the whole, sold into markets with worse road surfaces than those they were engineered for. Russian regional roads, African unsealed surfaces and Middle Eastern heat all accelerate the same failure modes: drop links and bushes first, then ball joints and wheel bearings, then shock absorbers. Cooling failures follow the heat rather than the mileage.

That failure sequence is predictable enough to plan inventory against, which is why our Chery coverage is weighted toward these categories rather than evenly spread.

Failure symptoms that identify the right part

  • Clunk over low-speed bumps — typically drop links or anti-roll bar bushes.
  • Knock when turning — outer tie rod end or, less commonly, the steering rack mount.
  • Vibration under braking — usually discs before they are visibly worn.
  • Temperature climb in traffic but not at speed — cooling fan assembly, electric thermostat or partially blocked radiator rather than the water pump.
  • Coolant loss with no visible leak — frequently the expansion cap or a heater core connection rather than a hose.

Each symptom maps to more than one candidate, which is why we confirm against VIN rather than shipping the first plausible reference.

Frequently asked questions

How many Chery suspension and cooling parts do you hold?

We list 178 Chery references across Suspension & Steering, Cooling System. Suspension and steering account for the largest share, followed by cooling system components.

Should suspension parts be replaced in pairs?

Yes for most steering and suspension linkages, because replacing one side produces uneven geometry and a pull. Where a reference should be ordered as a pair we note it on the quotation.

Do you supply radiators and condensers as well as hoses?

Both. Cooling system coverage includes radiators, condensers, thermostats, water pumps, cooling fan assemblies and associated hoses and clips.

What warranty applies?

Twelve months from bill of lading date as standard on stocked items. Specific terms appear on each quotation.
