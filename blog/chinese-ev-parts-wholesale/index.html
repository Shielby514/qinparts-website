What EVs still consume

  • Braking. Regenerative braking reduces but does not eliminate friction brake wear, and many EV owners report longer pad life paired with more caliper corrosion, because the friction brakes are used less often.
  • Suspension and steering. Battery packs add mass. A comparable EV hatchback can weigh three hundred kilograms more than its combustion equivalent, accelerating wear in bushes, ball joints, drop links and wheel bearings.
  • Thermal management. EVs need cooling for battery, motor, inverter and cabin. Electric compressors, electronic water pumps, coolant control valves and radiators replace their belt-driven counterparts and are not interchangeable with them.
  • Body and lighting. Accident frequency is unchanged, and EV body panels often carry sensors and radar mounts that make the part-specific subsidiary numbering more granular.

Why Chinese EVs are hard to source against

Traditional aftermarket catalogues are organised around engine displacement and production year. An electric platform has no displacement, and manufacturers update the drivetrain every eighteen months rather than every six years. The result is that the catalogue falls further behind the parc than it ever did for combustion vehicles.

Add the naming problem — a model sold under one name in China and another abroad — and identification becomes the bottleneck rather than price. This is the gap Qinparts was built to close: we maintain the reference across both names and quote against VIN.

What we do not supply

We do not quote traction batteries or other high-voltage assemblies from stock. Those are handled case by case and subject to transport classification, which varies by destination country. Everything mechanical and thermal-adjacent is quotable from the catalogue.

Frequently asked questions

Which Chinese EV parts do you stock?

We hold 151 part numbers applicable to Chinese-built electric and plug-in platforms, concentrated in Cooling System, Suspension & Steering, Body Parts, Brake Parts — braking, suspension, thermal management and body — rather than combustion-specific service items.

Do you sell traction batteries?

No. High-voltage assemblies are not held as stock because transport classification varies significantly by destination country. The mechanical and thermal-adjacent components quotable from the catalogue are listed above.

Are EV parts interchangeable with combustion equivalents?

Mechanical items sometimes are, within a shared platform. Thermal management components almost never are: electric compressors and electronic water pumps replace belt-driven units and have no combustion counterpart.

How do you confirm a part fits my vehicle?

Send the VIN and we validate the reference before quoting. Where the VIN is not available, photographs of the part and its markings plus basic measurements usually resolve it.
