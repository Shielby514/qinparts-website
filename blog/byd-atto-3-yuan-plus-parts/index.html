The BYD Atto 3 and the BYD Yuan Plus are the same vehicle. BYD sells it as the Yuan Plus in China and as the Atto 3 in export markets including Europe, Thailand, Australia, Brazil and Israel. Parts interchange between the two names in almost every case, which matters because a distributor quoting against a Chinese-market reference may find no result at all in a catalogue built for European imports.

Qinparts lists 37 OE part numbers applicable to this platform across Cooling System, Suspension & Steering, Body Parts, Filters. Cataloguing both names against one page removes the most common failure point in sourcing it: the part exists, but the buyer searched under the wrong name.

Electric drivetrain, conventional parts demand

An electric vehicle removes a long list of service parts — oil filters, spark plugs, timing components, exhaust — but it does not reduce the demand for anything mechanical. The Atto 3 carries the full complement of brake, suspension, steering, cooling and body components, and it is heavier than a comparable combustion hatchback, so suspension wear and tyre wear arrive sooner rather than later.

What does appear is demand for high-voltage-adjacent items: the electric compressor, electronic brake vacuum pump, reduction gearbox oil seal range, and the thermal management components that service both cabin and battery. These are not interchangeable with combustion equivalents and are poorly represented in general aftermarket catalogues.

Why the naming problem costs money

A distributor searching an international catalogue for "Atto 3 brake pads" frequently returns nothing, because the catalogue was populated from Chinese-market data under "Yuan Plus". The workshop then either waits for a dealer back-order or fits something approximate. Both outcomes lose the sale and, worse, lose the customer. We keep both names against one reference set so that either search resolves to the same part.

Confirming fitment on a BYD

BYD VIN structure encodes the model and production plant, and it is the most reliable way to resolve applicability. Send the VIN with your enquiry and we validate the reference before quoting. Where the VIN is unavailable, photographs of the part plus its measurements usually suffice.

Frequently asked questions

Are BYD Atto 3 and Yuan Plus parts identical?

They are the same vehicle under two names: Yuan Plus in China, Atto 3 for export. Parts interchange across the two. We list 37 references against both names so either search resolves correctly.

Do you supply high-voltage or battery components?

We supply the mechanical and thermal-adjacent components — electric compressor, electronic brake vacuum pump, reduction gearbox seals, cooling and thermal management — and quote the rest on request. Traction batteries are quoted case by case.

Which markets do you ship BYD parts to?

Principally Europe, Brazil, Thailand, Australia, Israel and Mexico, broadly the markets with the largest new-energy export parc. Freight is quoted to your port on FOB, CIF or EXW terms.

Can you identify a BYD part from a photo?

Yes. The OE number is fastest, but photographs of the part and its markings, plus basic measurements, are a workable starting point where the label is unreadable.
