Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from overseas distributors about ordering Chinese-brand auto parts wholesale from Qinparts.

Home › FAQ

What is your minimum order quantity?

MOQ depends on the part. Fast-moving service parts are quoted by carton; low-turnover or high-value items may carry a piece-level minimum. We state the applicable MOQ on each quotation rather than applying a blanket figure.

Can you identify a part if I only have the VIN or a photo?

Yes. OE number is fastest, but VIN, clear photographs of the part and its markings, or physical measurements are all workable starting points. This is the most common way overseas workshops begin an enquiry with us.

Do you sell genuine parts or aftermarket?

Both, and we label which is which. We do not present aftermarket parts as genuine. Where a part is genuine-service or OE-supplier sourced, the quotation says so explicitly.

What are your payment and shipping terms?

Standard terms are T/T with deposit against confirmed order and balance against bill of lading copy, with other structures available for established accounts. Shipments move on FOB, CIF or EXW as the customer prefers.

Which markets do you already ship to?

Primarily Russia and the CIS, the Middle East and Gulf, Africa, South and Central America, and Southeast Asia - broadly the regions with the largest export parc of Chinese-brand vehicles.

Do you sell to end consumers?

No. We supply wholesale only, to distributors, import-wholesalers, workshop chains and fleet operators.

Need a quotation for Chinese-brand parts?

Send the OE number, or send the VIN and a photo. We confirm applicability and quote with specification, MOQ, lead time and freight to your port.
