Ordering and minimum quantities

What is your minimum order quantity?

MOQ depends on the individual part, not the vehicle. Fast-moving service parts are quoted by carton; low-turnover or high-value items may carry a piece-level minimum. The applicable figure is stated on each quotation rather than applied as a blanket rule, so send the part numbers you need and we will return the MOQ per line.

Can I mix brands and categories in one order?

Yes, and it is usually how customers order. Parts across Chery, Geely, BYD, Jetour, Exeed and other Chinese marques consolidate into a single shipment, so a distributor stocking several brands pays one freight event rather than several.

How do I request a quotation?

Send the OE number, or the VIN together with the quantity and destination port. Where the OE number is unavailable, photographs of the part and its measurements are a workable substitute. The RFQ form collects exactly these fields.

Identifying the right part

Can you identify a part from the VIN alone?

Yes. The VIN is decoded against the relevant Chinese manufacturer catalogue and returns the applicable OE references. It is the most reliable starting point where the old part carries no readable stamp, and it is how the majority of overseas enquiries begin.

What if I only have a photo and measurements?

Clear photographs of the part and any visible markings, plus basic dimensions, are enough to resolve most enquiries. This route is slower than quoting an OE number and occasionally needs a second round of photos, which we will ask for specifically rather than guessing.

Do you provide OE cross-references?

Every part page lists interchange numbers alongside the primary OE reference, and each quotation confirms applicability against your VIN before dispatch. Cross-references are the core of what we do, because most international catalogues omit them for Chinese brands entirely.

Quality, sourcing and warranty

Do you sell genuine parts or aftermarket?

Both, and each quotation labels which is which. We do not present aftermarket parts as genuine. Where a part is genuine-service or OE-supplier sourced, the quotation states so explicitly, because distributors reselling into their own markets need to know exactly what they are buying.

What warranty applies?

Twelve months from bill of lading date as standard on stocked items. Where a different term applies to a specific part, it appears on the quotation rather than being assumed.

How do you control quality before shipment?

Every order is checked against the quotation specification before packing: part number, quantity, axle position, material or compound grade where specified, and packaging condition. Photographic pre-shipment evidence is available on request.

Payment and shipping

What payment terms do you work on?

Standard terms are T/T with deposit against confirmed order and balance against bill of lading copy, with other structures available for established accounts. The applicable terms are stated on each quotation.

Which incoterms and shipping modes do you support?

Shipments move FOB, CIF or EXW as the customer prefers, from Guangzhou or Nansha. Consolidated sea and rail options serve Russia, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and Gulf destinations, quoted to your port.

How are bulky body parts freighted?

Bumpers, panels and lamp assemblies are bulky rather than heavy, so body-part freight is quoted on volumetric weight and separately from unit price. Cartons are specified for export handling with individual protection for machined and painted surfaces.

Brands, markets and who we sell to

Which Chinese brands do you cover?

Chery, Geely, BYD, Jetour, Exeed, Jaecoo, Denza and Fang Cheng Bao are the marques with live part numbers in our catalogue today, plus Great Wall / Haval, MG, Changan, JAC and FAW enquiries handled against the same reference set.

Which markets do you ship to?

Principally Russia and the CIS, the Middle East and Gulf, Africa, South and Central America, and Southeast Asia — broadly the markets carrying the largest export parc of Chinese-brand vehicles.

Do you sell to end consumers?

No. We are wholesale-only, supplying import-wholesalers, national and regional distributors, workshop chains and fleet operators. There is no retail counter.

Need a quotation for Chinese-brand parts?

Send the OE number, or send the VIN and a photo. We confirm applicability and quote with specification, MOQ, lead time and freight to your port.
