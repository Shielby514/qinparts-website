Shipping &amp; Logistics

Consolidation, packing and freight terms for wholesale auto parts shipments from Qinparts.

Home › Shipping & Logistics

Consolidation

The economics of Chinese-brand parts distribution are driven by freight density. We consolidate across brands and categories into a single shipment, so a customer stocking eight brands is not paying eight separate freight events.

Packing standards

Cartons are specified for export handling: individual protection for machined and painted surfaces, reinforced board for dense items such as discs and hubs, and palletisation or crating where destination handling requires it.

Volumetric weight on body parts

Bumpers, panels and lamp assemblies are bulky rather than heavy. We quote body-part freight on volumetric weight and separately from unit price, so the landed cost is visible before the order is confirmed.

Documentation

Commercial invoice, packing list with carton-level detail, certificate of origin where required, and any destination-specific documentation are prepared with the shipment.

Ports and transit

Shipments primarily move from the Pearl River Delta port cluster. Transit time and routing are quoted per destination at the time of order.

Need a quotation for Chinese-brand parts?

Send the OE number, or send the VIN and a photo. We confirm applicability and quote with specification, MOQ, lead time and freight to your port.
