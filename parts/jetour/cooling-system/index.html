Questions about Jetour cooling system parts

Can you identify a part for a Jetour cooling system if I only have the VIN?

Yes. Send the VIN of the Jetour cooling system or a clear photo of the part and its markings, and we will confirm the OE number before quoting. This is the most common way overseas workshops start an enquiry with us.

What is the minimum order for Jetour cooling system parts?

MOQ depends on the individual part, not the vehicle. Fast-moving service parts are quoted by carton; low-turnover or high-value items may carry a piece-level minimum. The applicable MOQ is stated on each quotation rather than applied as a blanket figure.

Are these genuine parts or aftermarket?

Both are available and each quotation labels which is which. We do not present aftermarket parts as genuine. Where a part is genuine-service or OE-supplier sourced, the quotation states so explicitly.

Need a quotation for Chinese-brand parts?
