About Qinparts

A Guangzhou-based wholesale exporter built around one problem: identifying Chinese-brand part numbers outside China.

Home › About Qinparts

Guangzhou Qinparts Auto Parts Co., Ltd. is a Guangzhou-based export supplier of replacement parts for Chinese-brand passenger cars and light commercial vehicles. The company consolidates parts sourcing for Chery, Geely, BYD, Jetour, Exeed, Jaecoo, Denza and Fang Cheng Bao into single consolidated shipments for overseas importers, distributors, workshop chains and fleet operators. We are wholesale-only and do not sell retail.

Why Chinese-brand parts are hard to source

Most international parts catalogues were built around Japanese, Korean, European and American vehicle platforms. Chinese brands entered those catalogues late and unevenly, so workshops outside China frequently cannot identify a part number at all, let alone price it. That gap — not price — is usually what blocks a repair.

What we do about it

Qinparts maintains a working reference of OE numbers, interchange data and model-year applicability across the major Chinese export brands, and quotes against that reference rather than asking the customer to identify the part alone. Three identification routes are supported: the OE number, the VIN, or photographs and physical measurements of the part. Applicability is confirmed against the VIN before dispatch.

Who we sell to

Our customers are parts import-wholesalers, national and regional distributors, workshop chains, fleet maintenance operators and, in some markets, public-transport and municipal fleets. Every part number we publish is quotable, with specification, minimum order quantity, packing, lead time and freight terms stated per line rather than as generic defaults.

Quality position

Parts are supplied as genuine-service or OE-supplier sourced where available and as quality aftermarket where it is not. Each quotation labels which is which. We do not present aftermarket parts as genuine, because distributors reselling into their own markets need to know exactly what they are selling. For further detail see our quality control process and shipping standards.

Naming conventions worth knowing

Chinese vehicles frequently carry different names in domestic and export markets: the BYD Atto 3 is the BYD Yuan Plus in China, the Geely Coolray is the Binyue domestically. Our catalogue resolves both names against one part-number set, because otherwise a correct reference is missed simply for having been searched under the other name. Related marques share platforms too — Jetour, Exeed, Jaecoo and Omoda are Chery Group brands.
