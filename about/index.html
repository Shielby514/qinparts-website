About Qinparts

Qinparts is a Guangzhou-based export supplier of replacement parts for Chinese-brand passenger cars and light commercial vehicles.

Home › About Qinparts

Who we are

Qinparts operates as the export arm of Guangzhou Qinparts Auto Parts Co., Ltd.. We consolidate parts sourcing for Chinese-brand vehicles - Chery, Geely, BYD, Haval, MG, Changan, JAC and FAW - into single consolidated shipments for overseas distributors, import-wholesalers and fleet operators.

Why Chinese-brand parts are hard to source

Most international parts catalogues were built around Japanese, Korean, European and American vehicle platforms. Chinese brands entered those catalogues late and unevenly, so workshops outside China frequently cannot identify a part number at all, let alone price it. That gap - not price - is usually what blocks a repair.

What we do about it

We maintain a working reference of OE numbers, interchange data and model-year applicability across the major Chinese export brands, and we quote against that reference rather than asking the customer to identify the part alone. Where an OE number is unavailable, we can identify by VIN, by photograph, or by physical sample measurement.

Who we sell to

Our customers are parts import-wholesalers, national and regional distributors, workshop chains, fleet maintenance operators and, in some markets, public-transport and municipal fleets. We are a wholesale supplier and do not sell retail.

Need a quotation for Chinese-brand parts?

Send the OE number, or send the VIN and a photo. We confirm applicability and quote with specification, MOQ, lead time and freight to your port.
