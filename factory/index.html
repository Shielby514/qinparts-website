Manufacturing &amp; Supply Base

How Qinparts sources, consolidates and ships parts for Chinese-brand vehicles - and what that means for lead time and consistency.

Home › Manufacturing & Supply Base

A consolidated supply model, not a single factory

Chinese-brand aftermarket parts are produced by a fragmented base: some components come from the same tier-one suppliers that feed the vehicle assemblers, others from dedicated aftermarket manufacturers, others as genuine service parts. No single factory can cover a full brand range. We therefore operate as a consolidator across a vetted supplier base rather than as a manufacturer of everything we sell.

Supplier qualification

Suppliers are qualified on documented production capability, material certification for friction and rubber components, and batch-level traceability. Repeat suppliers are re-reviewed on accumulated claim data; a supplier with rising claim frequency loses allocation.

Consolidation and container planning

Because our customers typically buy across eight brands and eight categories at once, the practical constraint is freight, not unit price. We build mixed containers with carton-level packing lists, and consolidate to full container load wherever a customer’s order volume allows.

Lead time reality

Catalogue items ship from stock within days; non-stock items move to production with a stated window. We quote the real window rather than a best case, because a distributor planning a container needs the actual number.
