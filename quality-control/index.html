Quality Control

Inspection, specification control and claim handling for wholesale parts orders at Qinparts.

Home › Quality Control

Inspection before shipment

Every order is checked against the quotation specification before packing: part number, quantity, axle position, material or compound grade where specified, and packaging condition. Photographic pre-shipment evidence is available on request.

Specification on the quotation itself

Most quality disputes are specification disputes. We therefore state the attributes that actually determine whether a part fits and lasts - compound grade on friction material, core construction on radiators, bearing specification on hubs, voltage and pin count on sensors - on the quotation, not just a catalogue number.

Packaging for export

Parts are packed for container transit rather than domestic handling: individual protection for machined and painted surfaces, reinforced cartons for heavy items, and palletisation or crating where the destination port-handling environment warrants it.

Claims

Claims are handled against the original quotation specification and the pre-shipment record. Where a claim is confirmed as our error, we resolve by credit or replacement on the following shipment so the customer is not carrying dead stock while a dispute runs.

Need a quotation for Chinese-brand parts?

Send the OE number, or send the VIN and a photo. We confirm applicability and quote with specification, MOQ, lead time and freight to your port.
